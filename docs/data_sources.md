# Data source and access

The active study uses **BOXRR-23 only**, from the official mirror `cschell/boxrr-23` pinned to revision `af25be3ef76b176fbee0a094e82d97a611f9c950`. The dataset citation and owner page are in the [README](../README.md).

The operator confirmed access authority and authorized official intake, local filtering and private preparation on 5 November 2026. This operational record is not evidence that a recipient institution's ethics approval was supplied. Users must independently check applicable [dataset terms and use agreement](https://rdi.berkeley.edu/metaverse/boxrr-23/) before access, use or sharing. Do not attempt to re-identify users or publish raw or derived recordings.

The frozen expanded corpus contains 600 fitting, 101 development and 122 sealed final users. The cohort is selected and filtered; it is not a representative sample of all BOXRR-23. Raw recordings, derived human data, participant manifests and weights are excluded from Git. The exact cohort cannot be recreated from the public repository alone. [Setup](boxrr_study/setup.md) records preprocessing, sampling and provenance; [protocol](protocol.md) describes the split and evaluation boundaries.
